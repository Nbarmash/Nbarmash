Questions

 

Is it really free?

Yes. Coatwright is free to use right now. Paid extras such as cloud sync are planned for later, and the core quoting will stay simple and affordable.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. Open it in your phone's browser and start. To use it like an app, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone, or "Install app" on Android.

Can I use my own prices?

Yes. Set your labor rate, markup, minimum job, tax, deposit, paint and material costs, and how fast you work. Every quote uses your numbers.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Make a backup from Settings now and then, especially before switching phones.

Does it do commercial jobs?

Yes: offices, retail, restaurants, medical, hotels, apartments, warehouses, schools, churches, gyms and more.
